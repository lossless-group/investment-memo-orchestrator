## Why Care?

The [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) asks very little of a folder of markdown: parseable frontmatter, a
non-empty `type` on every doc, and a table of contents in `index.md`. Meet
that, and any OKF tool can read the folder without knowing anything about how
we work.

Context-v is now an OKF profile, and the rule that makes it one is a single
field: every doc declares a `type`, which is the name of its folder. This repo
holds the largest `context-v/` in the batch, 111 docs, and every one now
follows the rule.

## What's New?

- **103 docs gain one line**, `type: <Folder>`, at the end of their
  frontmatter. Eight already had it (the connector spec, its plans, and its
  loop). Nothing else in any doc moved, and no body line changed.
- The types, by folder: `Specs` (27), `Issue-Resolution` (24), `Reminders`
  (13), `Blueprints` (12), `Issues` (11), `Plans` (10), `Explorations` (2),
  `Prompts` (2), `Docs` (1), `Handoffs` (1).
- `issue-resolution/` keeps its name. It isn't one of the canonical folders,
  but renaming it would break links, so its docs are typed `Issue-Resolution`.
- `context-v/index.md` declares `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists all eleven
  folders.
- Each folder has its own `index.md`, one line per doc, using the doc's lede
  as its description, or the title alone when a doc has no lede.

## Under the Hood

This is a compliance change, not a content change, so the docs keep their
dates. `date_modified`, `date_authored_current_draft`, and the version fields
are exactly as they were. Every edit was made by hand, one doc at a time; no
script wrote frontmatter.

The repo qualified because `development`, `main`, and `master` were all at the
same commit, so this lands on all three at once. Part of the tree-wide sweep
planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
