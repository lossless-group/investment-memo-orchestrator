## Why care?

An investment memo's whole claim is that it read the room. This one wasn't.

The dataroom agent ran first, classified 158 documents, extracted financials,
cap table, team, traction, competitive, and 109 legal records — and then the
writer, which runs later in the same graph, built every section out of
Perplexity results. The extraction fed the analysis artifacts and nothing else.

You could see it plainly in one sentence of the ProfileHealth draft:

> *"Existing Investors: Previous investors and their participation in this round
> are not identified in available materials."*

Seven signed SAFEs, with investor names, amounts, caps and discounts, were in
`5b-legal-terms.json` in the same directory.

## What changed

**`writer.py` reads the dataroom.** A new `dataroom_facts_for_section()` selects
the slice each section can be answered from — financing instruments and cap table
for Funding & Terms, team for Organization, competitive and traction for
Opportunity — and threads it into both writer paths. The block states that the
dataroom outranks web research and names the failure directly: do not write that
something is "not disclosed" when it appears below.

It passes **per-document records, never the reconciled summary**, because
reconciliation collapses seven instruments at three caps into one number and
files the differences as conflicts.

Grounding is additive and cannot raise. Verified against real states for
portfolio companies and pipeline deals alike — `None`, key-absent, and malformed
all return `""`, so a deck-and-web deal behaves exactly as before.

**Time-series transcription reaches the pipeline.** `from_dataroom.py` converts
extractor output into `Observation`s and the dataroom step calls it. CleatusAI
produced the first pipeline-generated grid: 44 observations, 11 dense months,
four metrics, zero gaps, every column of the spec present.

**The transcriber computes nothing.** `time-series_analyst` became
`time-series_transcriber`; roll-up, `declare_metric_kind()` and `is_rolled_up`
moved out to a separate, unbuilt agent. A derived row in a transcription cannot
be told from a transcribed one, which destroys the provenance the artifact exists
to carry.

**Extractors stopped hiding their own failures.** Four did raw `json.loads` on a
completion, so a fenced-but-valid reply and a dead API call printed the same
message — and neither triggered the CLI→API fallback that `LLMResponse.ok`
enables. The cap table this concealed held all seven ProfileHealth SAFEs.

**Every step writes as it goes.** The dataroom pass held eight extractors in
memory and saved once at the end; a run killed at minute 20 left an empty
directory. And an unchanged dataroom is now reused rather than re-extracted —
109 documents were being re-read to write a new memo version.

## What is still wrong

Extraction truncates spreadsheets to 6–8k characters. The Unnatural Products
operating model extracts to 720,696 — the extractor sends 8,000, sees the cover
matter, and honestly reports "Data values not visible in provided extract."

| Unnatural Products | observations |
|---|---|
| hand adapter reading the workbook | 11,919 |
| pipeline, via the extractor | 0 |

The transcriber is not the bottleneck. Six companies were run today; one produced
a series, and it was the one whose spreadsheet was small enough to fit in a prompt.

Next: route spreadsheets sheet-by-sheet instead of truncating the workbook, and
narrow `LEGAL_DOCUMENT_TYPES` so 76 incorporation certificates stop being asked
for a valuation cap.
