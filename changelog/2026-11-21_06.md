```
feat(connector): Rewrite revised sections with draft.rewrite, from research and request only

section_reviser becomes draft.rewrite, the step both start_revision paths
reopen in place of draft.section. Its inputs are exactly the section's research
and its current text, and the partner's request leads its instruction. It says
to use no knowledge of its own and do no web research.

Steps gain a revision_only flag: such a step is never in a memo's first walk
and is handed out only when a revision reopens it. draft.section goes back to
first drafts only, and carries the same no-outside-knowledge line.

Also included: spec rows CONN-REV-01/02/03/06 and CONN-STEP-11/12 updated, the
First mapping and step-registry tables, start_revision's docs, plan step 1.
```

## Why Care?

The spec's Plan 9 says that, until the orchestrator runs on the server, Claude handles a research change as two separate tasks: edit the research file, then rewrite the section from it. Keeping them apart stops the writer drifting into research, keeps research checkable on its own, and made iterations tighter and faster. The connector now hands out a rewrite step whose instruction says so, so a subagent given only the writing step can't wander.

Before this, a revision reopened `draft.section`, the first-draft step. It saw its own first draft but not the section as the partner last read it, after tables, citations and fact check had changed it.

## What's New?

- **`src/connector/steps/draft.rewrite.md`** (order 55, optional, `revision_only: true`, `source_agent: src/agents/section_reviser.py`). It reads `research.section:@section` and `section.current:@section`, needs the research approved, and saves to `sections/{section}.rewrite.md`. Its output is a `section` artifact, so it becomes the section's current text for compile.
- **Revisions reopen it.** `flow.REOPEN_SECTION_STEPS` now lists `draft.rewrite` where `draft.section` was. A `start: research` revision hands out the research, then the rewrite; `start: draft` starts at the rewrite. `cancel_revision` drops a rewrite first made during the revision, so the section's earlier text is current again.
- **Exactly its inputs.** For a `revision_only` step, `gather_inputs()` adds no previous submission and no revision materials. New evidence reaches the rewrite only through the research. `with_revision()` points it at "the section as it stands".
- **`revision_only` in the step schema.** `StepDef.revision_only`, read from frontmatter and documented in the spec's §The step registry. `applicable()` uses it instead of a hardcoded step id.
- **`draft.section`** (instruction version 2) writes first drafts only. It says: "Use no knowledge of your own and do no web research: write only from your inputs."
- **Tests.** `tests/connector/test_rewrite.py` covers CONN-STEP-11 (three tests) and CONN-STEP-12. `test_revisions.py` now expects the rewrite in CONN-REV-01, 02, 03 and 06. CONN-ENH-02 and the two scripted fixture-co walks leave `revision_only` steps out of first-walk expectations, as they already left out `materials.brief`. The VP Eng and operator approved that test-intent change.

## What Isn't Tested

- **A real Claude following the instructions.** The tests check the instruction text and the inputs handed out, not whether a model actually keeps out outside knowledge.
- **Revisions with `start: research` and `material_ids`.** The rewrite doesn't get the materials. It relies on whoever does the research step putting them into the research. Nothing checks that the research really used them.
- **Plan 9's light `edit_section` path**, which doesn't exist yet. When it's built it may reopen `draft.rewrite` on its own.
