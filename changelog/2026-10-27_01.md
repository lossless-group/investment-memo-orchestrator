## Why care?

Founder-market fit says whether a founder *can* build the company. It doesn't
say whether this is the thing they'll spend their life on, or whether anyone on
the team understands why customers actually buy. The 12Ps memo also had no
place to discuss how the company reaches customers and what that plan assumes.

## What changed

- **People (P7)** now asks about:
  - **Founder Opus:** is this the founder's life's work, and are they one of
    the few people who could and would build it?
  - **Market empathy:** does someone on the team have an intuitive sense of
    how customers behave and why they buy?
  - **Enabling network:** what the advisors and investors around the team make
    possible.
  - **Cap table halo effect:** whether reputable investors raise the company's
    standing with customers, recruits, and future investors.
- **Process (P8)** gains a Go-To-Market subsection covering the GTM model and
  its assumptions (proven vs. still assumed), the growth engine (product-led
  growth, optimized funnel, growth flywheel, K-Factor, organic growth), and
  LTV over CAC. A "money printer" runs well beyond 3, as high as 15-20.
- **Progress (P11)** asks for pipeline value and how much of it has closed.
- **Funding & Terms** asks about cap table conviction: will the reputable
  investors already on the cap table follow on?
- The Organization section's length target rises from about 750 to 1,000
  words to fit all of this. The outline moves to version 1.1.0.
- **Every outline's preferred research domains** now include `tracxn.com` and
  `startuply.vc`, except a section that deliberately sets none.

## Found on the way

- The writer prompt reads only the first five `required_elements` of a
  section, and never reads the outline's global `vocabulary` block. The new
  items sit at the top of each list and are restated in the section
  description and questions, which the writer does read.
- `tools/validate_outlines.py` looks for its schema in `tools/` and checks two
  outline files that no longer exist. Nine outlines also fail the schema as it
  stands (no `guiding_questions` on dimension sections, and outline types the
  schema doesn't list). None of that is new in this change.
