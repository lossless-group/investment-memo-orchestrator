Final drafts no longer give a dataroom document several citation numbers.

merge guarantees one definition per source whatever path assembled the draft.

The financial extractor scores pages and reads the statements and notes, not
the first 8,000 characters; cli/extract_financials.py re-runs only that step.
A null share class no longer crashes the cap table step, and a rebuilt
analysis no longer nests legal terms.

Also included: consider-mode outline wording sends questions to the DDQ;
three open issues logged (citation collision across sections, no DDQ
artifact, successive datarooms); PIPELINE-REFERENCE regenerated.

## Why Care?

A memo's citations are how an analyst checks it. When one document appears as `[^57]`, `[^90]` and `[^116]`, a reader can't tell the memo leans on one source three times, and the citation list looks three times as well-sourced as it is. On the first deal where this was measured, 125 citation definitions were really 79 distinct sources.

Dataroom-heavy deals were hit hardest. Their sources are local files, and the financial extractor was reading only the first pages of each set of audited accounts: the cover, the contents and the directors' statement. A dataroom with five years of audited accounts came back with no figures.

## What's New?

- **One definition per source.** `_source_identity()` in `src/agents/citation_assembly.py` recognised a source by its `http(s)` link only. Dataroom citations are `file://` links, so they fell back to comparing the full definition text, which differs from section to section. Identity now covers `file://` links and percent-decodes them.
- **A guard at assembly.** New `merge_duplicate_definitions()` is the last step before `assemble_citations()` writes the final draft. It merges definitions that share a source and renumbers citations 1..N by first appearance. Tests: `tests/test_citation_dedupe.py`.
- **The financial extractor reads the statements.** It scores each page for how much of the extraction schema it can answer, sends the best pages, and orders documents by the latest period they report. `cli/extract_financials.py` re-runs only this step against a finished dataroom analysis. Tests: `tests/test_financial_page_selection.py`.
- **Two dataroom crashes fixed.** A shareholder row with a null share class or investor type no longer stops the cap table step. A rebuilt analysis now unwraps `5b-legal-terms.json` before the legal report reads it.
- **Consider mode keeps questions out of the memo.** The Decile Group outline asked the Flags section for "Specific diligence items to resolve flags". It now asks for the open flags stated as facts; questions go to the deal's due diligence questionnaire.

## Still Open

Three issues are logged in `context-v/issues/` and not yet fixed:

- **`Final-Draft-Citations-Collide-Across-Sections.md`.** Each section's research numbers its sources from 1, but `renumber_citations()` treats keys as global. The fix is to assemble with `consolidate_citations()`.
- **`Consider-Mode-Has-No-Due-Diligence-Questionnaire.md`.** The design for a collector that gathers questions into a DDQ artifact.
- **`Deals-With-Successive-Datarooms-Have-No-First-Class-Support.md`.** How to support a deal whose company sends a second, revised dataroom.

Also included: `docs/PIPELINE-REFERENCE.md` regenerated for the new CLI tools.
