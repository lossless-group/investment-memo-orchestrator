## Why Care?

The context-vigilance kit has a baseline frontmatter template: `title`,
`date_created`, `date_modified`, `site_uuid`, `hex_code`, `authors`, with
`authors`, `augmented_with`, and `tags` written as lists and `status` in
Title-Case. Every doc that follows it can be sorted, linked, and rolled up
the same way as every other doc in the tree.

This pass fills only what's missing, and only from facts. Nothing is
guessed, no value changes meaning, and no date is set to the day of the
sweep.

## What's New?

- **Six docs gain a `site_uuid` and `hex_code`**, minted by command and
  checked for duplicates across the whole tree first: the company-timeline
  blueprint, the archive-dating hitlist, the visual-collector crop issue,
  the tidyverse reminder, the targeted legal scan spec, and the time-series
  analyst spec.
- **Three docs gain `authors: [Michael Staton]`**, the only human who ever
  committed them: the information-hierarchy blueprint, the Tauri getting
  started doc, and the changelog voice reminder.
- **87 docs write `augmented_with` as a one-item list** instead of a bare
  string. The model string is unchanged, quotes and all.
- **Six statuses move to Title-Case:** `In Progress` becomes `In-Progress`
  (four connector plans and the outline reminder), and `proposed` becomes
  `Proposed` (the study-migration plan). Each says what it said before.

## Under the Hood

Every edit was made by hand, one doc at a time; no script wrote
frontmatter. No `date_modified`, `date_authored_current_draft`, or version
field moved.

Left as they were, on purpose:

- The study-migration plan still has a legacy `date:` key. It can't be
  renamed, because the doc already has both `date_created` and
  `date_authored_initial_draft`, and a rename would duplicate one.
- Two tags that look lowercase are correct as written: `Table-of-Contents`
  (a minor word) and `macOS` (a product name).
